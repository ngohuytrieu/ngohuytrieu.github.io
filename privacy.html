Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what data Social Uploader ("the app", "we") handles when you connect your TikTok account and other social accounts, how it is used, and how to delete it.

Where the app runs

Social Uploader runs on your own computer. We do not run a server that receives your videos, account data or tokens. All data described below is stored in a local database and folder on the computer where you run the app.

Data the app handles

From TikTok, after you click Allow:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access token and refresh token, used only to upload and publish the videos you choose.
  • The privacy options your account allows and the status of each upload, used to post with the visibility you picked and to show progress.

From you:

  • The video files you upload and the title or caption you type.
  • Credentials for the other platforms you connect (YouTube, Facebook Page, Threads, Zalo).

The app does not collect your followers, messages, likes, comments, viewing history, contacts or location, and it does not collect any data about other people.

How the data is used

Only to do what you ask: connect your accounts, upload and publish your videos, and show the results. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we never sell it.

Sharing

Your video and caption are sent only to the platforms you select for each upload, through their official APIs. Those platforms handle that content under their own policies, such as the TikTok Privacy Policy. We share nothing with anyone else.

Storage and retention

Tokens and settings stay in the local database until you disconnect the account or delete the app's files. Uploaded videos stay in the local upload folder until you delete them. We keep no copy of any of it.

Deleting your data

  • Revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions, then remove Social Uploader. Revoke other platforms in their own account settings.
  • Delete the app's local database and upload folder to remove every stored token and video.
  • Videos you already posted stay on TikTok until you delete them in TikTok.
  • For help deleting your data, email trieu.ngohuy@gmail.com.

Security

Tokens are sent to the platforms only over HTTPS and are stored only on your computer. Keep that computer secure, because anyone with access to it can use the connected accounts.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age TikTok requires in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
