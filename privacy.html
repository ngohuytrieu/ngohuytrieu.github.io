Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Social Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to their own Facebook Page, TikTok, YouTube, Threads and Zalo accounts.

Data we collect

The app stores only the access tokens needed to post to the owner's accounts, on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share any data about other people.

Data deletion

To delete your data, remove the app from your account settings on each platform. All stored tokens are deleted from the owner's computer on request.
